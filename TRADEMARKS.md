# Trademarks

The code is MIT and the art, writing and data are CC BY 4.0, so you may fork,
change and redistribute govSim. The **name and logo** are not covered by those
licenses.

- **Forks and modified versions must use a different name and logo**, and must not
  suggest they are the official game.
- You may say your project is "based on govSim" or "a fork of govSim".
- Unmodified builds and links to the official project may use the name to refer to it.

This keeps players able to tell the official, ad-free, tracking-free, nonpartisan
game apart from repackaged versions. Questions: open an issue.
