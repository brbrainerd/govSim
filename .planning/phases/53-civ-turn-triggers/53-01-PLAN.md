# Plan 53-01: Civ-Style Per-Turn Trigger Cadence & Smart Turn Blocker Stamp

**Phase:** 53
**Requirements:** `TRIG-01`, `TRIG-02`, `TRIG-03`

## Objective
Prevent single-turn cognitive overload by introducing a deterministic Civ-style per-turn spotlight trigger cadence (`src/app/turn-triggers.ts`), enriching `Session.stagedActionsSummary` with explicit per-turn clearing statuses (`will_clear`, `at_risk`, `unsent`), and upgrading `StatusBar.svelte` and Advisor cards with 1-click staging and live clearing counts.

## Tasks
1. Create `src/app/turn-triggers.ts` with `resolveTurnTrigger` and `buildTurnChecklist` to compute at most one spotlight trigger per turn (deferring non-urgent Task Force prompts when a Crisis is active or on Turn 1 inauguration) and a 3-slot pending/clearing checklist.
2. Enrich `StagedActionItem` in `src/app/session.svelte.ts` with `status: 'will_clear' | 'at_risk' | 'unsent'`, `statusLabel: string`, and `detail?: string` so every queued decision knows whether it will clear at turn end, fail a floor vote, or sit unsent.
3. Update `src/ui/StatusBar.svelte` to surface live clearing badges (`CLEARS ✓`, `PASSING ✓`, `SHORT VOTES ⚠`, `UNSENT !`) in the staged decisions popover and under the `Next turn` stamp.
