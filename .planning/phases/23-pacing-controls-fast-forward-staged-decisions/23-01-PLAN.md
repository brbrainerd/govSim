# Plan 23-01: Staged Decisions Summary, Fast-Forward Engine Loop & StatusBar Pacing UI

## Objective
Implement `stagedActionsSummary`, `canFastForward`, and `fastForwardToNextEvent()` in `src/app/session.svelte.ts`, wire the Staged Decisions pill/popover and `"Skip to event"` button in `src/ui/StatusBar.svelte` and `src/screens/Game.svelte`, and verify with unit tests and Playwright E2E tests across `1376x768` and `1280x720`.

## Tasks
1. **Session Pacing & Staged Actions State (`src/app/session.svelte.ts`)**:
   - Define `StagedActionItem` (`id`, `label`, `screen`, `warning`).
   - Add `stagedActionsSummary` derived property inspecting `this.draft` against `this.current`.
   - Add `hasBlockingEvent` and `canFastForward` derived properties.
   - Add `fastForwardToNextEvent()` method that advances up to 8 turns, halting immediately on any crisis, critical deadline (`turnsRemaining <= 1`), midterm/election turn, or impeachment event.
2. **StatusBar Staged Decisions Tray & Fast-Forward UI (`src/ui/StatusBar.svelte`, `src/screens/Game.svelte`)**:
   - Render `[data-testid="staged-decisions-pill"]` and toggleable `[data-testid="staged-decisions-popover"]` in `StatusBar.svelte` with 1-click screen jump links.
   - Render `[data-testid="fast-forward-btn"]` (`"Skip to event"`) in `StatusBar.svelte` next to `"Next turn"`.
3. **Unit & Playwright E2E Verification (`src/app/session-pacing.test.ts`, `tests/pacing-controls.spec.ts`)**:
   - Unit test `stagedActionsSummary` (unsent budget warning, sent bill, executive orders) and `fastForwardToNextEvent()` halting on crises/deadlines.
   - E2E test Staged Decisions popover and Skip to Event button across `1376x768` and `1280x720` with axe and screenshots.
