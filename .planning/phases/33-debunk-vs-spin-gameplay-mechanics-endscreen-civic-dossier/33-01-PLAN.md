# Phase 33 Plan: "Debunk vs. Spin" Gameplay Mechanics & EndScreen Civic Dossier

## 1. Goal & Requirements
Give civics myth cards meaningful strategic gameplay teeth by introducing a pure-engine "Debunk vs. Spin" mechanic. Players can choose to **Debunk** a myth (granting +5 Clout for institutional realism) or **Spin** it on television (granting a short-term +2% presidential approval rally, lasting -1.5 public trust penalty, and +5 heat in Evil Mode). Term-end civic performance is immortalized in a dedicated **Civic Mastery Dossier** on `EndScreen.svelte` with unlockable badges ("Truth Teller" and "Demagogue").

- **Requirements**:
  - `SPIN-01`: Pure-engine `spinMyth?: string` support in `PowerDecisions`, registered coefficients `mythSpinApprovalBoost`, `mythSpinTrustPenalty`, `mythSpinHeatDelta`, `'event:myth_spin'` ledger modifier with `why.ts` copy, save validation, and bit-identical preservation of all 8/8 determinism hashes.
  - `SPIN-02`: Wire `"Spin This Myth"` in `MythModal.svelte` and `Session`, and render a responsive, viewport-safe Civic Mastery Dossier in `EndScreen.svelte` tracking Myths Busted vs. Myths Spun.

## 2. Tasks

### Task 1: Pure-Engine `spinMyth` Architecture & Validation (`SPIN-01`)
- **Files**:
  - `src/engine/coefficients.ts`: register `mythSpinApprovalBoost` (2.0), `mythSpinTrustPenalty` (-1.5), `mythSpinHeatDelta` (5).
  - `src/engine/powers.ts`:
    - Add `spinMyth?: string` to `PowerDecisions`.
    - Add `spunMyths?: readonly string[]` and `heatDelta?: number` to `PowerDecisionsResult`.
    - Implement spin resolution in `resolvePowerDecisions`: validate myth exists in `MYTHS_CATALOG`, is not already viewed or spun, append to `nextViewedMyths` and `nextSpunMyths`, add 1-turn approval rally modifier and permanent trust penalty modifier, generate log entry, and return `heatDelta`.
  - `src/engine/game.ts`:
    - Add `readonly spunMyths?: readonly string[]` to `GameState`.
    - Apply `pRes.spunMyths` to `interimState.spunMyths` conditionally to keep golden hashes bit-identical when no myths are spun.
    - Factor `pRes.heatDelta` into Evil Mode `nextHeat` calculation.
  - `src/content/text/why.ts`:
    - Add `'event:myth_spin': 'Populist spin on national television'`.
  - `src/app/save.ts`:
    - Validate `powers.spinMyth` against `getMyth()` in `validateDecisions`.
  - `src/engine/powers.test.ts`:
    - Add unit tests verifying debunk vs spin mechanics, modifier generation, evil heat delta, and prevent duplicate spin.
