<objective>
Make the engine draw every random number from a keyed, stateless stream and use arithmetic only, so no choice can move another draw and the same inputs replay bit-identically.

Purpose: FND-01 (no-decision player faces exactly autopilot's shocks, D2) and FND-02 (bit-reproducible math, D10). This is the foundation every later Phase 1 plan and Phases 2-8 build on; the characterization snapshot it records guards the refactors in plans 01-05 to 01-07.
Output: rng.ts, detmath.ts, hash.ts, replayFixture.ts, src/testing/scan.ts, an arithmetic-only game.ts with report.luck, and tests (shocks, rng, purity, determinism incl. characterization).
</objective>
