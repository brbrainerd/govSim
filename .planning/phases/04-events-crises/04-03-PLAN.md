  - Top: Red `"BREAKING"` ribbon banner.
  - Left column:
    - Label `"What happened"`.
    - Headline `{crisis.definition.headline}`.
    - If `crisis.definition.type === 'endogenous'`: badge `"Autopilot didn't face this"` in alert/ink tint.
    - `<ReporterScene category={crisis.definition.id} />`.
    - Link: `How it works: {crisis.definition.explainerTitle}` (disabled button).
  - Right column:
    - Headline `"What will you do?"`.
    - Radiogroup of option cards:
      - Option label and brief description.
      - Clout cost pill (`{opt.cloutCost} clout` or `free`).
      - Disabled if player lacks clout (`opt.cloutCost > session.clout`).
      - Keyboard selectable with Space/Enter or Arrow keys.
    - Primary action: Red stamp button `"DECIDE"`:
      - Saves choice to `session.selectCrisisOption(crisis.definition.id, selectedOptionId)` and calls `onClose()`.
