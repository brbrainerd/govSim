# Plan 20-01: Theme Switching Hookup & Evil Mode Endgame Engine

## Objective
Connect reactive theme switching (`applyTheme('evil')` / `applyTheme('day')`) across App and Game flows, ensure color tokens and contrast comply with WCAG AA, and build the pure arithmetic endgame evaluation engine for Kleptocrat Heist Tiers and corrupt historian verdicts.

## Requirements Covered
- **UI-01**: Satirical visual reskin applying a dark Americana theme (oxblood, aged gold, ink) when Evil Mode is active.
- **UI-03**: Specialized End Screens ranking unconvicted players by their offshore heist tier, or detailing their downfall upon conviction or electoral rejection (engine tier & verdict logic).

## Tasks
1. **Engine Logic (`src/engine/evil-endgame.ts` & `src/engine/units.ts` / `src/engine/coefficients.ts`)**:
   - Define heist tier thresholds in `units.ts` / `coefficients.ts`:
     - `TIER_PETTY_GRIFTER_MAX = 10` ($B)
     - `TIER_GILDED_OLIGARCH_MAX = 25` ($B)
     - `TIER_SOVEREIGN_PLUNDERER_MAX = 50` ($B)
   - Implement `classifyKleptocratVerdict`:
     - If `convicted`: Tier "DISGRACED CONVICT", summary of total asset forfeiture and federal sentencing.
     - If `reelectionLost`: Tier "OUSTED GRIFTER", summary of electoral rejection and whether loot remains untouched or tied up in grand jury subpoenas.
     - If survived term unconvicted:
       - `< 10B`: "PETTY GRIFTER" (Swiss vaults, low-key escape)
       - `10B - 25B`: "GILDED OLIGARCH" (Megayacht, private enclave)
       - `25B - 50B`: "SOVEREIGN PLUNDERER" (Bought islands, diplomatic immunity)
       - `>= 50B`: "TRANSNATIONAL KLEPTOCRAT" (Global shadow empire, sovereign plunder masterclass)
   - Ensure pure arithmetic calculation, zero bare literals, strict party-blindness.
   - Comprehensive unit tests in `src/engine/evil-endgame.test.ts`.
2. **Reactive Theme Hookup (`src/App.svelte` & `src/design/tokens.ts`)**:
   - In `src/App.svelte`: reactively trigger `applyTheme(session?.isEvilMode ? 'evil' : 'day')` when `session` changes or on unmount/restart.
   - Verify token contrast and ensure dark theme variables (`--paper`, `--card`, `--ink`, `--alert`, `--gold`, etc.) look sharp and pass WCAG AA contrast rules.
   - Verify `applyTheme('day')` is restored on restart or exit.
