# Plan 18-02: Pure Evil Lever Resolution Engine & Trace Attribution (LEVER-01, LEVER-02, LEVER-03)

## Objective
Implement pure deterministic execution logic for enacting corrupt executive levers, calculating aggregate siphons, clout deductions, heat adjustments, and trace-backed penalties to approval and trust in `src/engine/evil.ts` and `src/engine/game.ts`.

## Scope
1. **Resolution Function (`src/engine/evil.ts`)**:
   - `EvilLeversResult`:
     - `cloutCost`: total clout spent
     - `stashDeltaBn`: total kickback added to offshore stash
     - `heatDelta`: net change to heat (+heat from siphons, -heat from suppressions)
     - `approvalDelta`: net change to presidential approval
     - `trustDelta`: net change to public trust
     - `budgetDeltaBn`: direct deficit impact (e.g. from inflated crony contracts)
     - `enactedLevers`: list of enacted lever IDs
     - `logEntries`: log entries explaining actions taken
   - `resolveEvilLevers(state: GameState, requestedLevers: readonly EvilLeverId[]): EvilLeversResult`:
     - Filters for affordability (does current clout cover total cloutCost? If not, rejects or executes affordable subset).
     - Clamps heat outcomes cleanly within `[MIN_HEAT, MAX_HEAT]`.
2. **Integration into `src/engine/game.ts` `step()`**:
   - Update `EvilDecision` interface in `src/engine/evil.ts` and `src/engine/game.ts` to accept `enactedLevers?: readonly EvilLeverId[]`.
   - Execute `resolveEvilLevers` during `step()`.
   - Debit clout, update offshore stash, compute next heat with both quarterly decay and lever deltas.
   - Record ledger terms: `clout:evil`, `approval:evil_levers`, `trust:evil_levers`.
   - Register trace keys and user-facing explanations in `src/engine/trace.ts` and `src/content/text/why.ts`.
3. **Determinism & Unit Tests**:
   - Add unit tests in `src/engine/evil.test.ts` for lever resolution, clout boundary handling, and heat capping.
   - Update golden hashes and save test fixtures if needed.
