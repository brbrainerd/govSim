# Plan 18-01: Corrupt Executive Levers Catalog & Engine Coefficients (LEVER-01, LEVER-02, LEVER-03)

## Objective
Define the corrupt executive action catalog in `src/data/evil.ts` and register all associated kickback rates, clout costs, heat generation/suppression deltas, and institutional fallout in `src/engine/coefficients.ts` and `src/engine/units.ts`.

## Scope
1. **Catalog Definition (`src/data/evil.ts`)**:
   - `EvilLeverId`:
     - `'crony_contracts'`: No-bid defense and infrastructure contracts to favored donors.
     - `'auction_public_lands'`: Fire-sale mineral and drilling rights on federal public land.
     - `'sell_tariff_exemptions'`: Executive carve-outs from tariffs for corporate campaign contributors.
     - `'solicit_library_donations'`: Foreign oligarch and corporate contributions to presidential library foundation.
     - `'fire_inspectors_general'`: Summary dismissal of agency watchdogs investigating administration kickbacks.
     - `'pressure_justice_department'`: Direct interference in DOJ and FBI prosecutorial decisions.
   - `EvilLever`: Metadata including name, short title, description, satirical flavor quote, category (`'siphon'` | `'suppression'`), cloutCost, kickbackBn, heatDelta, trustPenalty, approvalPenalty, budgetCostBn.
2. **Coefficients & Constants (`src/engine/coefficients.ts`, `src/engine/units.ts`)**:
   - Register all values in `C`:
     - `evilCronyKickbackBn`, `evilCronyHeatDelta`, `evilCronyCloutCost`, `evilCronyTrustCost`, `evilCronyApprovalCost`, `evilCronyBudgetCostBn`
     - `evilLandKickbackBn`, `evilLandHeatDelta`, `evilLandCloutCost`, `evilLandTrustCost`, `evilLandApprovalCost`
     - `evilTariffKickbackBn`, `evilTariffHeatDelta`, `evilTariffCloutCost`, `evilTariffTrustCost`
     - `evilLibraryKickbackBn`, `evilLibraryHeatDelta`, `evilLibraryCloutCost`, `evilLibraryTrustCost`
     - `evilFireIgHeatSuppression`, `evilFireIgCloutCost`, `evilFireIgTrustCost`, `evilFireIgApprovalCost`
     - `evilDojHeatSuppression`, `evilDojCloutCost`, `evilDojTrustCost`, `evilDojApprovalCost`
   - Constants in `units.ts`: `EVIL_LEVER_IDS` array and type guards.
3. **Unit Tests**:
   - Add unit tests validating catalog completeness, ID uniqueness, and coefficient sanity in `src/data/evil.test.ts`.
