# Plan 19-03: Reactive Session Store, Impeachment Modal & Backlash UI (IMPEACH-02, IMPEACH-03)

## Objective
Build the satirical modal dialog `src/ui/ImpeachmentModal.svelte` presenting the House impeachment vote tally and Senate trial verdict, wire state to `src/app/session.svelte.ts`, and display special counsel active alerts in `src/ui/StatusBar.svelte`.

## Scope
1. **StatusBar Alerts (`src/ui/StatusBar.svelte`)**:
   - When `evilState.specialCounselAppointed` is true:
     - Display active badge: `SPECIAL COUNSEL SUBPOENAS ACTIVE (-25 CLOUT/QTR)`.
2. **Impeachment Modal (`src/ui/ImpeachmentModal.svelte`)**:
   - Triggers when House passes Articles of Impeachment or when Senate trial concludes.
   - Vote Breakdown:
     - House Vote: e.g. 235 Yeas, 200 Nays (Articles of Impeachment Adopted).
     - Senate Trial Vote: e.g. 68 Guilty, 32 Not Guilty (Two-Thirds Supermajority Reached).
   - If Convicted:
     - Dramatic satirical proclamation of removal from office.
     - "All offshore accounts frozen by federal decree ($0.0B recovered)".
     - Term Over button routing to End screen.
   - If Acquitted:
     - "Acquitted by narrow minority: Heat burns hot but power remains intact".
   - Token-pure styling and accessible modal dialog (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`).
3. **Session Wiring (`src/app/session.svelte.ts`, `src/screens/Game.svelte`)**:
   - Reactive `showingImpeachmentModal` state on session.
   - Mounting in `src/screens/Game.svelte`.
4. **Component Tests**:
   - Add unit tests in `src/ui/ImpeachmentModal.test.ts`.
