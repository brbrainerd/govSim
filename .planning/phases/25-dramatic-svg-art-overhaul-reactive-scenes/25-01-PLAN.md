<action>
1. Check `src/design/tokens.ts`: ensure all needed illustration tokens exist in `base` and `themes.evil` (all colours in `.svelte` files MUST use `var(--...)` — zero `#hex` or `rgb(` literals in `.svelte` files).
2. Upgrade `src/ui/art/Character.svelte` while preserving backward compatibility for all existing props (`skin`, `hair`, `suit`, `tie`, `pose`, `talking`, `flip`, `pearls`) and adding:
   - `mood?: 'confident' | 'stressed' | 'scheming' | 'panicked' | 'triumphant'` (default `'confident'`)
   - `hairstyle?: 'parted' | 'swept' | 'bun' | 'cropped'` (default `'parted'`)
   - `glasses?: boolean` (default `false`)
   - `prop?: 'none' | 'folder' | 'coffee' | 'gavel' | 'mic'` (default `'none'`)
   - Extended `pose?: 'stand' | 'point' | 'desk' | 'wave'` (default `'stand'`)
3. Render rich SVG layers inside `<svg viewBox="0 0 120 175" data-mood={mood} data-pose={pose}>`:
   - **Torso & Tailored Suit**: Contoured shoulders, notched lapels (`<path>` collar/lapel fold lines), crisp white shirt collar V-neck, knotted tie with tie-clip (or multi-bead pearl necklace when `pearls` is true), pocket square accent, and sleeve cuffs.
   - **Head & Facial Anatomy**:
     - Left and right ears with inner ear contour lines at `y=48..62`.
     - Contoured head with subtle chin/jawline curve and cheek shadow overlay (`opacity=".12"`).
     - Distinct hairstyle paths for `'parted'` (classic side-part with volume wave), `'swept'` (pompadour/executive sweep), `'bun'` (editorial crown bun + side sweeps), and `'cropped'` (textured crop).
     - Nose bridge and tip contour (`M60 46 L63 55 L58 56`).
     - Optional horn-rimmed `glasses` (`<rect>` frames + bridge at eye level).
   - **5 Reactive Mood Expressions**:
     - `'confident'`: Angled composed eyebrows, sclera + pupil + white eye catchlight, warm confident smile curve.
     - `'stressed'`: Inward furrowed eyebrows, under-eye fatigue lines, tense wavy mouth, and a visible temple sweat droplet (`data-testid="char-sweat"`).
     - `'scheming'`: Half-lidded upper eyelids, one arched eyebrow + one lowered eyebrow, asymmetric sly smirk.
     - `'panicked'`: High arched eyebrows, wide eyes with small pupils, open oval mouth, and two sweat droplets.
     - `'triumphant'`: High curved eyebrows, bright eyes, broad beaming smile, and a small gold star/sparkle accent near the lapel/head.
   - **Poses & Props**:
     - Support `'stand'`, `'point'`, `'desk'`, and `'wave'` arm positions, plus optional hand-held props (`'folder'`, `'coffee'`, `'gavel'`).
</action>
