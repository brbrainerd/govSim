<task>
<task_name>1. Implement pure Electoral College simulation model in src/engine/voters.ts</task_name>
<description>
Create `src/engine/voters.ts`:
- Define `StateVoterState`, `ElectoralSummary`, and `MidtermResult` types.
- Load baseline state margins from `data/us-2026.json` and district margins from `src/data/electoral.ts`.
- Implement `calculateTippingPointOffset()`: computes the baseline margin of the 270th electoral vote unit so both Democratic and Republican presidents start balanced at 270 EV (D4, GR-16).
- Implement `calculateElectoralCollege(state: GameState, party: 'democrat' | 'republican')`:
  - Computes each state's and district's current player margin:
    $$\text{Margin}_i = \text{BaselineMargin}_i + (\text{approval} - 50) \times \text{elasticity} + \text{exposureAdjustment}_i + \text{shock}_i$$
  - Sourced industry exposures (farm, manufacturing, energy) modulate margins based on tariffs, GDP growth, and inflation.
  - Determines winner ('you' | 'them') for each of the 56 units.
  - Computes player, opponent, and toss-up electoral votes (summing to 538).
  - Identifies swing states ($|\text{margin}| \le 5.0$) and the tipping-point state.
  - For turn $\ge 9$ (Year 3+), computes honest projection range ($\pm 1.28 \sigma_{\text{rem}}$).
- Pure function, deterministic, no UI dependencies.
</description>

<task_name>2. Implement midterm seat shifts and death-spiral bounding in congress.ts and game.ts</task_name>
<description>
Update `src/engine/congress.ts` and `src/engine/game.ts`:
- Add `CongressState` or update `groups` to support dynamic seat tallies.
- Implement `calculateMidtermShift(approval: number, seed: number, turn: number)`:
  - House net change: $(\text{approval} - 50) \times 1.2 - \text{penalty}$.
  - Strictly bound House loss between $-35$ and $+15$ seats to prevent runaway death spirals (D3, GR-18, GR-19).
  - Senate net change: bound between $-6$ and $+4$ seats.
  - Distribute seat changes across the 5 House ideological blocs proportionally.
- In `src/engine/game.ts`:
  - When transitioning at turn 8 (Year 2 Fall) and turn 24 (Year 6 Fall), invoke midterm resolution.
  - Record midterm log entry in `report.log`: `{ kind: 'midterm', id: 'midterm_results', detail: string, amount: netSeatShift }`.
</description>

<description>
Create `src/engine/voters.test.ts`:

- **GR-14 (2024 Replay Test)**: Unadjusted 2024 baseline reproduces Trump 312 EV and Harris 226 EV, with ME-2 won by R and NE-2 won by D.

- **GR-18 & GR-19 (Midterms & Bounded Divergence Test)**:
  - House seat loss in midterms never exceeds $-35$ seats even at low approval.
  - Comeback test: Starting from 30% approval at turn 6, sensible play recovers to $\ge 42\%$ approval by turn 16 in $\ge 50\%$ of seeds.
