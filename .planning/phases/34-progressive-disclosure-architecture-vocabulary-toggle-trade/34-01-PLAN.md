### Task 2: Wire Persistent `vocabMode` (`Plain Talk` vs `Policy Wonk`) in `Session`, `save.ts`, `NewGame.svelte`, and `StatusBar.svelte` (`VOCAB-01`)
<read_first>
- `src/app/session.svelte.ts`
- `src/app/save.ts`
- `src/screens/NewGame.svelte`
- `src/ui/StatusBar.svelte`
- `src/screens/Game.svelte`
</read_first>

<action>
1. In `src/app/session.svelte.ts`:
   - Import `type VocabMode` from `../content/glossary`.
   - Add `vocabMode?: VocabMode` to `GameOptions`.
   - Add reactive `vocabMode = $state<VocabMode>(...)` on `Session` defaulting to `'plain'` (reading `localStorage.getItem('govsim_vocab_mode')` or `options.vocabMode`).
   - Add `toggleVocabMode(): void` and `setVocabMode(mode: VocabMode): void` that update `this.vocabMode`, `this.options`, `localStorage`, `document.documentElement.dataset.vocab`, and `storeAutosave(this)`.
2. In `src/app/save.ts`:
   - Preserve optional `vocabMode: 'plain' | 'wonk'` in `parseSave`.
3. In `src/ui/StatusBar.svelte` & `src/screens/Game.svelte`:
   - Pass `vocabMode={session.vocabMode}` and `onToggleVocab={() => session.toggleVocabMode()}` from `Game.svelte` to `StatusBar.svelte`.
   - Add a compact toggle button (`data-testid="vocab-mode-toggle"`, `aria-pressed={vocabMode === 'wonk'}`) in `.party-calm-wrap` showing `🗣️ Plain Talk` in `'plain'` mode and `📜 Policy Wonk` in `'wonk'` mode.
4. In `src/screens/NewGame.svelte`:
   - Add a `vocabMode` toggle (`data-testid="vocab-mode-toggle-newgame"`) alongside `unlockAll` and `livingWorld` in a compact 3-column settings grid so vertical height actually decreases and `1376×768` / `1280×720` remain 100% scroll-free.
</action>
