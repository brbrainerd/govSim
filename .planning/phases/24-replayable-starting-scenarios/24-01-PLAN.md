# Plan 24-01: Replayable Starting Scenarios Catalog, NewGame Selector, Briefing Banner & EndScreen/Share Integration

## Objective
Implement the 4 starting scenarios (`standard_2026`, `stagflation_shock`, `debt_brinkmanship`, `global_trade_war`), add the scenario selector to `NewGame.svelte`, surface the active scenario banner/objective on `OvalOffice.svelte` and `EndScreen.svelte`, persist `scenario` in `SaveFile` and `share.ts`, and verify with unit tests and Playwright E2E tests across `1376x768` and `1280x720`.

## Tasks
1. **Scenarios Catalog & Content Gate (`src/content/scenarios.ts`, `src/testing/content-gates.test.ts`)**:
   - Define `ScenarioId`, `ScenarioDef`, `SCENARIOS`, `getScenario`, `isValidScenarioId`, and `applyScenarioStart(start, scenarioId)`.
   - Register `../content/scenarios` in `src/testing/content-gates.test.ts`.
2. **Session, Save & Share Integration (`src/app/session.svelte.ts`, `src/app/save.ts`, `src/app/share.ts`)**:
   - Add `scenario?: ScenarioId` to `GameOptions`, `SaveFile`, and `RunDescriptor`.
   - Apply `applyScenarioStart(start, options.scenario)` deterministically in `Session` constructor for both player `history` and `autopilot`.
   - Expose `session.activeScenario` derived getter.
3. **NewGame, OvalOffice & EndScreen UI (`src/screens/NewGame.svelte`, `src/screens/OvalOffice.svelte`, `src/screens/EndScreen.svelte`)**:
   - Render scenario selector cards (`data-testid="scenario-card-<id>"`) in `NewGame.svelte`.
   - Render scenario badge/briefing (`data-testid="oval-scenario-badge"`) on `OvalOffice.svelte` when a non-default scenario (or any scenario) is active.
   - Render scenario badge (`data-testid="end-scenario-badge"`) on `EndScreen.svelte`.
4. **Unit & Playwright E2E Verification (`src/content/scenarios.test.ts`, `tests/scenarios.spec.ts`)**:
   - Unit test `applyScenarioStart`, `Session` scenario overrides, and `encodeRunCode`/`decodeRunCode` round-trip with scenarios.
   - E2E test selecting `"70s Stagflation Shock"` on `NewGame.svelte`, verifying initial macro metrics and scenario badge on `OvalOffice.svelte`, across `1376x768` and `1280x720`.
