# Plan 14-01: Engine Modeling — Foreign Policy Levers, Sanctions & Coefficients

## Objective
Implement pure arithmetic modeling for defense readiness postures, foreign assistance scaling, and targeted economic sanctions with sourced macro coefficients and determinism guarantees.

## Requirements Covered
- **FORPOL-01**: Player can adjust defense readiness posture vs. budget savings and foreign assistance levels in the budget/executive screens.
- **FORPOL-02**: Player can enact targeted economic sanctions (asset freezes, trade restrictions) with explicit domestic inflation and trade fallout.

## Proposed Changes
1. **Coefficients (`src/engine/coefficients.ts`)**:
   - Add sourced coefficients for defense posture budget deltas:
     - `defenseSurgeCost`: +$35B annual deficit for heightened deterrence posture (CBO).
     - `defensePeacetimeSavings`: -$25B annual deficit for diplomatic peacetime drawdown (CBO).
     - `foreignAssistancePerPoint`: $0.45B annual deficit per point of foreign assistance (base 100 = ~$45B/yr, CRS R43838).
   - Add sourced coefficients for targeted sanctions fallout:
     - Asset Freezes (`asset_freeze`): +0.05% CPI inflation, -0.03% output gap.
     - Tech/Defense Export Controls (`export_controls`): +0.10% CPI inflation, -0.08% output gap.
     - Comprehensive Energy Embargo (`energy_embargo`): +0.25% CPI inflation, -0.15% output gap.
2. **Domain Engine (`src/engine/foreign.ts`)**:
   - Types:
     - `DefensePosture = 'peacetime' | 'standard' | 'surge'`
     - `SanctionType = 'asset_freeze' | 'export_controls' | 'energy_embargo'`
     - `SanctionDefinition`: id, name, description, legalBasis, cloutCost, inflationImpact, gapImpact, citation
     - `SANCTIONS_CATALOG`: 3 curated sanction types with statutory basis (IEEPA, 50 U.S.C. § 1701; Export Control Reform Act, 50 U.S.C. § 4811; Magnitsky Act)
     - `ForeignPolicyDraft`: `{ defensePosture?: DefensePosture; foreignAssistance?: number; activeSanctions?: readonly SanctionType[] }`
     - `ForeignPolicyState`: `{ defensePosture: DefensePosture; foreignAssistance: number; activeSanctions: readonly SanctionType[] }`
   - Evaluation function `resolveForeignPolicy(...)` computing net deficitBn, inflation, gap, and clout changes.
3. **Decisions & Autopilot (`src/engine/decisions.ts`)**:
   - Add `foreignPolicy?: ForeignPolicyDraft` to `Decisions`.
   - Update `autopilotDecisions()`: returns baseline foreign policy (`defensePosture: 'standard'`, `foreignAssistance: 100`, `activeSanctions: []`).
   - Validate and sanitize foreign policy input in `sanitizeDecisions()`.
4. **Game Loop (`src/engine/game.ts`)**:
   - Store `foreignPolicy: ForeignPolicyState` in `GameState`.
   - Initialize with standard baseline in `newGame()`.
   - In `endTurn()`, resolve foreign policy and apply quarterly impacts to debt, inflation, and output gap.
5. **Testing**:
   - Create `src/engine/foreign.test.ts` verifying all calculations, bounds, sanctions fallout, and determinism.
   - Run existing suite (`npm test`) to ensure zero regression across all 54 test suites.

## Verification
- Unit test suite: `npx vitest run src/engine/foreign.test.ts`.
- Full suite: `npm test && npm run check`.
