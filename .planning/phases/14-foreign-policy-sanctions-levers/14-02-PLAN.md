# Plan 14-02: Executive & Budget UI Integration — Defense Readiness & Sanctions Controls

## Objective
Connect foreign policy and sanctions to `session.svelte.ts`, render accessible defense readiness and targeted sanctions controls in `Powers.svelte`, and integrate foreign assistance controls in `Budget.svelte`.

## Requirements Covered
- **FORPOL-01**: Player can adjust defense readiness posture vs. budget savings and foreign assistance levels in the budget/executive screens.
- **FORPOL-02**: Player can enact targeted economic sanctions (asset freezes, trade restrictions) with explicit domestic inflation and trade fallout.

## Proposed Changes
1. **Session State (`src/app/session.svelte.ts`)**:
   - Add `foreignPolicy` to `DraftDecisions`:
     - `setDefensePosture(posture: DefensePosture): void`
     - `setForeignAssistance(level: number): void`
     - `toggleSanction(sanction: SanctionType): void`
   - Include `draft.foreignPolicy` in the `nextTurn()` decisions object.
   - Update `hasPendingDraft` and reset handling.
2. **Executive Screen (`src/screens/Powers.svelte`)**:
   - Add "Foreign Policy & National Security" section:
     - Defense Readiness Posture selector (Peacetime Drawdown / Standard Baseline / Forward Surge) with budget impact labels (-$25B / $0 / +$35B).
     - Targeted Economic Sanctions cards from `SANCTIONS_CATALOG` with toggle switches/buttons, clout badges (10 Clout), and explicit domestic inflation & GDP fallout callouts.
     - Accessible button roles, aria-pressed states, and keyboard navigation.
3. **Budget Screen (`src/screens/Budget.svelte`)**:
   - Render a dedicated "Foreign Assistance & Diplomacy" section/row or lever slider.
   - Displays current level (0% to 200%, default 100%), annual outlay (~$45B baseline), and explicit callout: "<1% of federal spending".
4. **Verification**:
   - Unit tests in `src/screens/Powers.test.ts` and `src/screens/Budget.test.ts`.
   - `npm run check` and `npm test`.
