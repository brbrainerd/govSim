# Plan 16-01: Engine Modeling — Trade Agreements, Sector Tariffs & Retaliatory Dynamics (TRADE-01, TRADE-02)

## Objective
Implement pure deterministic engine data structures, coefficients, and calculation functions for strategic sector tariffs, bilateral trade agreements, and foreign retaliatory tariff calculations.

## Scope
1. **Coefficients (`src/engine/coefficients.ts`)**:
   - Register sourced coefficients for trade dynamics:
     - `sectorTariffRevenue`: revenue per active sector tariff.
     - `sectorTariffPricePassThrough`: consumer price inflation per active sector tariff.
     - `tradeAgreementExportBoost`: GDP growth boost per bilateral agreement.
     - `retaliatoryTariffThreshold`: base tariff rate above which foreign partners retaliate.
     - `retaliatoryTariffScale`: retaliatory rate per point of excess US tariff.
     - `retaliatoryExportDrag`: GDP/output drag per point of foreign retaliatory tariff.
     - `retaliatoryAgDrop`: agricultural export demand reduction factor.
2. **Trade Engine (`src/engine/trade.ts`)**:
   - Types:
     - `SectorTariffId`: `'chips' | 'minerals' | 'cleanEnergy'`
     - `TradeAgreementId`: `'eu' | 'indoPacific' | 'americas'`
     - `TradeState`: `{ sectorTariffs: SectorTariffId[]; agreements: TradeAgreementId[]; retaliatoryRate: number; }`
     - `SECTOR_TARIFFS_CATALOG`: metadata, statutory basis (Section 301, Section 232), description, revenue, inflation.
     - `TRADE_AGREEMENTS_CATALOG`: metadata, partner bloc, clout cost, export boost, description.
   - Pure functions:
     - `defaultTradeState()`: baseline trade state.
     - `calculateRetaliatoryRate(tariffRate: number, sectorTariffs: readonly SectorTariffId[], agreements: readonly TradeAgreementId[])`: calculates effective foreign retaliatory tariff rate.
     - `calculateTradeBalance(tariffRate: number, tradeState: TradeState, gdp: number)`: calculates exports, imports, trade balance, consumer price pass-through.
     - `sanitizeTradeState(raw: unknown)`: validation and sanitization.
3. **Unit Tests (`src/engine/trade.test.ts`)**:
   - Test default state, catalogue integrity, retaliatory rate calculation, mitigation by trade agreements, and trade balance calculations.
