# Plan 16-02: Macro Engine Integration, Voter Coalition Shifts & Autopilot Calibration (TRADE-01, TRADE-02)

## Objective
Integrate the pure trade engine into the quarterly simulation loop (`step`), register trace prefixes, wire voter coalition shifts in trade-exposed states (`TRADE-02`), and verify property invariants, determinism, and balance.

## Scope
1. **Engine GameState & Decisions (`src/engine/game.ts`, `src/engine/decisions.ts`)**:
   - Extend `GameState` with `trade: TradeState`.
   - Extend `Decisions` with `trade?: { sectorTariffs?: SectorTariffId[]; agreements?: TradeAgreementId[]; }`.
   - Wire sector tariff revenues into deficit calculations.
   - Wire price pass-through, retaliatory export drag, and trade agreement export boost into `inf` and `g` ledgers.
2. **Trace & Plain Language (`src/engine/trace.ts`, `src/content/text/why.ts`)**:
   - Register `'trade:'` in `SOURCE_PREFIXES`.
   - Add concise plain-language translations in `whyText(source)` with reading grade <= 9.0.
3. **State Voter Coalitions (`src/engine/voters.ts`)**:
   - Factor foreign retaliatory tariff pressure into state-level approval for `TRADE_EXPOSED_STATES`.
4. **Calibration & Verification**:
   - Verify property invariants with 1000 randomized runs (`invariants.property.test.ts`).
   - Replay determinism suite (`determinism.test.ts`).
   - Regenerate `balance-report.json` and ensure all balance tests pass.
