# Plan 17-01: Pure Evil Mode Engine, Heat Decay, and Registered Coefficients (EVIL-01, EVIL-02, EVIL-03)

## Objective
Implement the pure arithmetic engine modules for Evil Mode, including the `EvilState` contract, quarterly heat decay and clamping, heat penalty calculations, and coefficient registrations.

## Scope
1. **Coefficients & Units (`src/engine/coefficients.ts`, `src/engine/units.ts`)**:
   - Register `heatDecayQuarterly` (quarterly natural heat dissipation).
   - Register `heatApprovalPenalty` (approval drag per point of heat).
   - Register `heatTrustPenalty` (public trust erosion per point of heat).
   - Register `heatCongressionalFriction` (legislative passage friction per point of heat).
   - Add unit constants `MAX_HEAT = 100`, `MIN_HEAT = 0` to `units.ts`.
2. **Pure Evil Engine Module (`src/engine/evil.ts`)**:
   - Define `EvilState` and `EvilDraft` interfaces.
   - `defaultEvilState()` returning zero stash and zero heat.
   - `calculateHeatDecay(currentHeat, decayRate)` returning decayed heat clamped to [0, 100].
   - `calculateHeatPenalties(heat)` returning approval delta, trust delta, and congress seat/vote hurdles.
   - `sanitizeEvilState(raw)` protecting against malformed or NaN inputs.
3. **Unit Tests (`src/engine/evil.test.ts`)**:
   - Test heat decay and clamping to [0, 100].
   - Test penalty calculations across heat spectrum (low, moderate, critical).
   - Verify zero bare literals outside exempt files via `npm run guard`.
