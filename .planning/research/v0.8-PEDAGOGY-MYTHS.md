## 1. Pedagogical Foundations & Research Synthesis

Why do traditional educational popups in strategy games feel forced ("chocolate-covered broccoli"), and what makes civic learning intrinsically motivating?

1. **Intrinsic Integration (*Habgood & Ainsworth, 2011*)**
   - Learning content must be embedded inside the core mechanics that give the player agency, not bolted onto the side as an encyclopedia quiz.
   - In `govSim`, players already hunt for policy levers to move **Approval**, **Clout**, **Inflation**, **Jobs**, **Deficit**, and **Prosperity Index**. Myth cards become fun when they are triggered by the player's own tactical hunger for a shortcut and immediately pivot them toward real constitutional levers.

2. **Productive Failure & Tempting "Mirage Levers" (*Kapur, 2008*; *Gee, 2003*)**
   - Players bring real-world civic misconceptions into the game ("Why can't I just cap gas prices?", "Why can't I order the Fed to cut rates?", "Why doesn't cutting foreign aid balance the budget?").
   - Placing tempting **"Mirage Levers"** (plausible populist "Easy Buttons") directly in the screen header/sidebar where a player expects them — or surfacing them in the Oval Office briefing when voter anger spikes — creates a moment of situated discovery at the exact point of player intent.

3. **Prediction Error, Curiosity Gaps & Refutation Texts (*Loewenstein, 1994*; *Bjork, 1994*; *Sinatra & Broughton, 2011*)**
   - Passively displaying "MYTH / REALITY" text leads to shallow skimming.
   - Asking the player to make an **optional 1-click prediction** ("Spot the bottleneck in the causal chain" or "What blocks this order?") before slamming down the **`MYTH BUSTED`** stamp activates prediction error and makes the refutation memorable.
   - **Zero-Friction Escape Hatch**: To prevent the mechanic from feeling like a mandatory school test, players can always click `"Got It"` / `"Skip to Reality"` directly without answering the prediction prompt.

4. **Situated Action & Immediate Transfer (*Gee, 2003*)**
   - Once a myth is busted, the player's natural question is: *"Okay, then what CAN I actually do?"*
   - Converting static `realLevers` pills in `MythModal.svelte` into **1-click tactical pivot buttons** (`realLeverActions`) that jump directly to the target screen and stage or highlight the real lever turns civic knowledge into immediate strategic action.

5. **Strategic Trade-Off: "Debunk vs. Spin" (*Incentive-Driven Satire*)**
   - Why do politicians repeat civic myths if they are false? Because demagoguery buys short-term applause at the expense of institutional trust!
   - Giving players a choice on each unviewed myth card between:
     - **Debunk / Statesman Path (`viewMyth`)**: Accept institutional reality for **+5 Clout** (`C.mythRewardClout`) and a 1-click tactical pivot to real policy levers.
     - **Spin / Demagogue Path (`spinMyth`)**: Peddle the populist myth on television for an immediate **+2% Approval rally** (`C.mythSpinApprovalBoost`, 1 turn) at the cost of **−2.5 Social Trust** (`C.mythSpinTrustPenalty`) (plus **+4 Heat** in Evil Mode).
   - This teaches *both* the constitutional reality and the political incentive structure behind public misconceptions, while remaining 100% nonpartisan (`GR-20`, `GR-21`).

## 3. Expanded 12-Card Spectrum-Balanced Myth Catalog (`|left - right| <= 1`)

Current 8 cards:
- `gas_prices` (`right`), `foreign_aid_deficit` (`right`), `supply_side_taxes` (`right`) → 3 `right`
- `rule_by_decree` (`left`), `debt_ceiling_default` (`left`), `trade_deficits` (`left`) → 3 `left`
- `interest_rates` (`both`), `presidential_jobs` (`both`) → 2 `both`

New 4 cards to reach **12 cards** (`4 left`, `4 right`, `4 both` → `|4 - 4| = 0 <= 1`):
1. `veto_line_item` (`both`, `institution` — Congress/Budget): *"The President can cross out wasteful lines in a bill before signing it."* → Reality: Line-item vetoes were struck down as unconstitutional (`Clinton v. City of New York`, 524 U.S. 417); the President must sign or veto the entire bill.
2. `popular_vote_mandate` (`left`, `institution` — Voters/Electoral College): *"Winning the national popular vote guarantees the presidency and a governing mandate."* → Reality: State electoral votes (270 threshold) elect the President, and narrow House/Senate margins still control legislation (`U.S. Const. art. II, § 1`).
3. `immigration_crime_budget` (`right`, `mechanism` — Social/Voters): *"Closing the border immediately solves federal entitlement deficits."* → Reality: Long-term deficits are driven by aging demographics and healthcare costs; working-age payroll taxpayers fund Social Security trust inflows (`CBO Demographic Outlook`).
4. `scotus_instant_reversal` (`both`, `institution` — Powers/Courts/Reforms): *"Appointing one new Justice lets the President immediately overturn any past Supreme Court ruling."* → Reality: The Court can only rule on live legal cases brought up through federal appeals courts over years (`U.S. Const. art. III, § 2`).

## 4. Pure Engine & Determinism Preservation

1. **Zero Impact on Existing Golden Replays**:
   - Existing golden hashes and unit tests only exercise `powers.viewMyth` (or no myth action).
   - Adding optional `readonly spinMyth?: string;` to `PowerDecisions` (`src/engine/powers.ts`) leaves all existing code paths and RNG streams untouched when `spinMyth` is undefined.
2. **State & Trace Integration**:
   - When `powers.spinMyth` is resolved in `resolvePowerDecisions`:
     - Verify `getMyth(spinId)` is valid and `!nextViewedMyths.includes(spinId)`.
     - Record `spinId` in `nextViewedMyths` (preventing double-dipping on the same myth) and emit a `LogEntry` with `kind: 'myth_spin'`.
     - Add a 1-turn `approval` boost modifier (`+C.mythSpinApprovalBoost`, source `'event:myth_spin'`, tag `'event'`) and a persistent `trust` penalty modifier (`-C.mythSpinTrustPenalty`, source `'event:myth_spin'`, tag `'event'`).
     - In Evil Mode (`state.evil`), also increase Heat or stage a cynical spin bonus.
   - Add `'event:myth_spin'` to `WHY_COPY` in `src/content/text/why.ts`.
   - Validate `powers.spinMyth` in `src/app/save.ts` (`validateDecisions`) and serialize it in `src/app/share.ts` if needed.
