# Requirements: govSim (Milestone v0.3)

**Defined:** 2026-10-12
**Core Value:** A player can make real presidential tradeoffs against an honest, explainable model of the current US, and always understand *why* the country got better or worse.

## v0.3 Requirements

Requirements for Milestone v0.3: Social Policy & Domestic Reform.

### Domestic Social Policy (SOC)

- [x] **SOC-01**: Healthcare policy levers (ACA subsidy levels, Medicare drug negotiation scope, public option) with health coverage, costs, and budgetary tradeoffs.
- [x] **SOC-02**: Immigration policy levers (border security funding, agricultural & high-skilled visa caps, status regularization) with labor supply, population, and wage impacts.
- [x] **SOC-03**: Energy & Climate policy levers (clean energy tax credits, federal leasing permits, carbon standards) with energy price, emissions, and regional industrial consequences.
- [x] **SOC-04**: Education policy levers (Title I federal aid, student loan repayment assistance, universal pre-K grants) with workforce productivity and fiscal outlays.
- [x] **SOC-05**: Sector and demographic distribution: social policy choices dynamically impact state-level voter coalitions, cost-of-living metrics, and congressional seat leanings.

### Structural Government Reform (REF)

- [ ] **REF-01**: Senate filibuster reform levers (maintain 60-vote threshold, talking filibuster requirement, 55-vote threshold, simple majority carve-outs) altering congressional legislative passability.
- [ ] **REF-02**: Supreme Court and judicial reform options (18-year term limits, statutory code of conduct, bench expansion) with institutional credibility and confirmation battles.
- [ ] **REF-03**: Electoral reform policies (ranked-choice voting grant incentives, nonpartisan redistricting commissions, small-dollar donor matching) shifting voter participation and competitive seats.
- [ ] **REF-04**: Constitutional amendment mechanism with high bar requirements: 2/3 House + 2/3 Senate passage, followed by 3/4 state legislature ratifications.

### Household Impact Persona (HOU)

- [ ] **HOU-01**: "Your Household" card integrated into the Oval Office and Economy screens displaying real-time household budget impacts (groceries, energy bills, health insurance premiums, income tax, wages).
- [ ] **HOU-02**: Preset household demographic personas (e.g., Rust Belt manufacturing family, Sun Belt retiree, suburban educator couple) and custom configuration (state, income bracket, household size).
