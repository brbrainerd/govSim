Phase 26 Plan 02 injects dry, nonpartisan institutional irony and humor into the Oval Office stage via a live news-ticker chyron (`src/content/chyrons.ts`) and witty advisor quips in `src/content/briefing.ts`, strictly gated by `src/testing/content-gates.test.ts`.

<task_name>Create satirical news chyron generator and register in content gates (src/content/chyrons.ts, src/testing/content-gates.test.ts)</task_name>
<read_first>
- src/testing/content-gates.test.ts
- src/content/briefing.ts
</read_first>
<action>
1. Create `src/content/chyrons.ts` exporting `CHYRON_POOL` and `pickChyron(session: Session): string`:
   - Write 18+ short, punchy, dryly ironic headlines (every sentence ≤ 16 words, short words so Flesch-Kincaid grade is ≤ 8.0) organized by condition:
     - `evil_high_heat`: e.g., `"CHYRON: White House shredder catches fire. Press secretary blames dry winter air."`
     - `evil_default`: e.g., `"CHYRON: Mysterious Cayman island named fastest growing town in America."`
     - `high_inflation`: e.g., `"CHYRON: Grocery stores now offer thirty-year mortgages on eggs and butter."`
     - `high_unemployment`: e.g., `"CHYRON: Economists say the job market builds character. Workers disagree."`
     - `high_debt`: e.g., `"CHYRON: Treasury mints new bonds. Bond traders ask for a bigger vault."`
     - `high_tariffs`: e.g., `"CHYRON: Port cranes take a long nap as tariff forms pile up."`
     - `high_approval`: e.g., `"CHYRON: Pundits shocked as voters briefly agree that things are fine."`
     - `low_approval`: e.g., `"CHYRON: Poll finds stray dog would lead the race in three swing states."`
     - `default`: e.g., `"CHYRON: Congress debates lunch menu for six hours. Calls it a productive week."`
   - Select deterministically based on `session.current.turn` and live indicators.
2. Add `'../content/chyrons'` to the `CONTENT_MODULES` list in `src/testing/content-gates.test.ts`.
3. Enhance `src/content/briefing.ts` bubbles with sharp, witty institutional observations that remain under 20 words/sentence and grade ≤ 9.0.
</action>
