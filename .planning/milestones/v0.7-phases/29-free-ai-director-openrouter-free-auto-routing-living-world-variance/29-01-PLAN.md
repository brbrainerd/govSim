     - `livingWorldGapShockMax`: `{ value: 0.35, unit: 'output gap pts per quarter', basis: 'judgement', range: [0.1, 0.8], note: 'max quarterly output gap variance in Living World mode' }`
     - `livingWorldInfShockMax`: `{ value: 0.2, unit: 'inflation pts per quarter', basis: 'judgement', range: [0.05, 0.5], note: 'max quarterly inflation variance in Living World mode' }`
     - `dynamicCrisisGapCap`: `{ value: 0.8, unit: 'max abs output gap delta from a dynamic crisis option', basis: 'judgement', range: [0.2, 1.5], note: 'safety clamp for AI dynamic crisis output gap impact' }`
     - `dynamicCrisisInfCap`: `{ value: 0.8, unit: 'max abs inflation delta from a dynamic crisis option', basis: 'judgement', range: [0.2, 1.5], note: 'safety clamp for AI dynamic crisis inflation impact' }`
     - `dynamicCrisisApprovalCap`: `{ value: 6, unit: 'max abs approval pts from a dynamic crisis option', basis: 'judgement', range: [2, 12], note: 'safety clamp for AI dynamic crisis approval impact' }`
     - `dynamicCrisisTrustCap`: `{ value: 5, unit: 'max abs trust pts from a dynamic crisis option', basis: 'judgement', range: [2, 10], note: 'safety clamp for AI dynamic crisis trust impact' }`
2. In `src/engine/types.ts` and `src/engine/decisions.ts`:
   - Add `DynamicCrisisDelta` interface: `{ gapDelta: number; infDelta: number; approvalDelta: number; trustDelta: number }`.
   - On `Decisions`, add optional `turnEntropy?: number` and `dynamicCrisisDelta?: DynamicCrisisDelta`.
   - In `validateDecisions` (`src/engine/decisions.ts`), validate:
     - If `turnEntropy !== undefined`: must be a finite integer in `[0, 0x7fffffff]`.
     - If `dynamicCrisisDelta !== undefined`: all 4 fields must be finite numbers within `[-C.dynamicCrisis*Cap, +C.dynamicCrisis*Cap]`.
3. In `src/engine/game.ts`:
   - Inside `step()`, only when `d.turnEntropy !== undefined` (so standard runs with `turnEntropy === undefined` execute zero extra draws and remain 100% bit-identical!):
     - Draw keyed normal shocks using `seed ^ d.turnEntropy` on stream `19` (`'living-world'`) and apply bounded output-gap and inflation impulses (`event:living_world_variance`), amplified when compound macro stress is present (e.g., high tariff + high inflation or high deficit).
   - When `d.dynamicCrisisDelta !== undefined`, record its clamped deltas into the `gap`, `inflation`, `approval`, and `trust` ledgers under source `'event:ai_dynamic_crisis'`.
4. In `src/content/text/why.ts`:
   - Add plain-language trace labels for `'event:living_world_variance'` and `'event:ai_dynamic_crisis'`.
</action>
