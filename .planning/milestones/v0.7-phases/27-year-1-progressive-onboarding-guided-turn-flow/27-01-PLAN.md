<action>
1. In `src/app/screens.ts`:
   - In `navFor(turn: number, unlockAll = false, isEvilMode = false)`, add `newlyUnlocked: !unlockAll && s.unlockTurn > 1 && turn === s.unlockTurn` to each screen entry.
   - Export `year1GuideFor(turn: number): { step: string; detail: string } | null` returning:
     - `turn === 1`: `{ step: 'Year 1 · Step 1 of 3', detail: 'Draft your first Budget, then whip 218 votes in Congress.' }`
     - `turn === 2`: `{ step: 'Year 1 · Step 2 of 3', detail: 'Unlocked: Your Powers & Trade. Issue an Executive Order or adjust tariffs.' }`
     - `turn === 3`: `{ step: 'Year 1 · Step 3 of 3', detail: 'Unlocked: Voters map. Check your 270 Electoral College path before Year 2.' }`
     - `turn >= 4`: `null`.
2. In `src/app/session.svelte.ts` and `src/app/save.ts`:
   - Add optional `unlockAll?: boolean` to `GameOptions` (validated as optional boolean in `parseSave`).
   - Add `unlockAll = $state<boolean>(false)` on `Session`, initialized from `Boolean(options.unlockAll)`.
   - Add `unlockAllScreens(): void` on `Session` that sets `this.unlockAll = true` and `this.options = { ...this.options, unlockAll: true }`.
</action>
