# Plan 18-03: Reactive Session Store & Satirical Executive Levers UI (LEVER-01, LEVER-02, LEVER-03)

## Objective
Provide the interactive user interface for corrupt executive actions in `src/screens/Powers.svelte` (or a dedicated component `EvilLeversSection.svelte`) when playing in Evil Mode, wired to `src/app/session.svelte.ts`.

## Scope
1. **Session Store Staging (`src/app/session.svelte.ts`)**:
   - Track staged evil levers in turn draft (`stagedEvilLevers: Set<EvilLeverId>`).
   - Helpers: `toggleEvilLever(id: EvilLeverId)`, `isEvilLeverStaged(id: EvilLeverId)`, `stagedEvilLeversCost`, `stagedEvilLeversKickback`, `stagedEvilLeversHeatDelta`.
   - Forward `stagedEvilLevers` into `EvilDecision.enactedLevers` when submitting turn.
2. **UI Component (`src/screens/Powers.svelte` or `src/ui/EvilLeversSection.svelte`)**:
   - Conditionally render when `session.isEvilMode` is true.
   - Categorized card grid:
     - Siphoning Levers (Crony Contracts, Land Auctions, Tariff Waivers, Library Foundation) with clear kickback $B badges and Heat warning badges.
     - Oversight Suppression Levers (Fire Inspectors General, Pressure DOJ) with Heat reduction and Trust penalty warnings.
   - Live Clout cost calculation with affordability indicators.
   - Visual toggle / enact buttons with satirical tooltips and feedback.
   - Keyboard accessible and full compliance with design tokens (oxblood, ink, paper, gold).
3. **Component Unit Tests**:
   - Unit tests in `src/screens/Powers.test.ts` verifying rendering in Evil Mode, suppression in Standard Mode, toggle interactions, and staging reactivity.
