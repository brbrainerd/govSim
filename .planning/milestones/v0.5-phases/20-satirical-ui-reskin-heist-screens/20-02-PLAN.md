# Plan 20-02: Oval Office / Situation Room Evil Mode Reskin & Live Widgets

## Objective
Implement the Evil Mode reskin and live status widgets in the Oval Office hub screen, integrating the live Offshore Stash counter, interactive Heat Gauge with investigation threshold indicators, satirical kleptocratic briefing copy, and quick-action routes to corrupt executive levers.

## Requirements Covered
- **UI-01**: Satirical visual reskin applying a dark Americana theme when Evil Mode is active.
- **UI-02**: Live Offshore Stash counter and Heat Gauge integrated into the status bar and Oval Office / Situation Room.

## Tasks
1. **Dynamic Kleptocratic Briefing (`src/content/briefing.ts`)**:
   - Support Evil Mode in `generateBriefing(session)`:
     - When `session.isEvilMode`:
       - High heat (>80): Urgent impeachment peril warnings.
       - Special counsel (>60): Subpoena defense warnings and clout preservation.
       - Heat rising (>40): Evidence leakage alerts.
       - Base state: Satirical kleptocracy briefing ("Public funds awaiting extraction. Review executive levers.", "Treasury extraction proceeds on schedule.").
2. **Oval Office Hub UI Updates (`src/screens/OvalOffice.svelte`)**:
   - In `OvalOffice.svelte`:
     - When `session.isEvilMode`:
       - Replace the standard Rosette score with an **Offshore Loot Rosette / Counter** displaying `session.current.evil.offshoreStashBn` in gold ($B) with delta from previous quarter.
       - Add a dedicated **Evil Situation Card** (`data-testid="evil-situation-card"`) displaying:
         - Current Heat Gauge (0–100) with visual meter, color-coded heat state (Cool, Simmering, Special Counsel at 60, Impeachment at 80), and numerical readout.
         - Current investigation status (None, Special Counsel Active, Articles of Impeachment Pending).
         - Quick action button (`Enact Corrupt Levers` -> `go('powers')`).
       - Ensure all layout containers honor `ScreenLayout` zones and fit cleanly across viewports without layout distortion or scrolling overflow.
3. **Unit Tests (`src/screens/OvalOffice.test.ts` or component tests)**:
   - Verify `OvalOffice.svelte` renders the evil score and evil situation card when `isEvilMode` is true.
