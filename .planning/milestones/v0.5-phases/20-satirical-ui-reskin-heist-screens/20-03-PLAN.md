# Plan 20-03: Satirical Heist & Downfall End Screen

## Objective
Adapt `src/screens/EndScreen.svelte` to provide a specialized, satirical kleptocratic end-of-game experience when Evil Mode was played: displaying the final Offshore Heist Tally, Kleptocrat Historian Verdict, Downfall Summary (upon Senate conviction or electoral defeat), and an Extraction Ledger detailing corrupt executive levers enacted.

## Requirements Covered
- **UI-01**: Satirical visual reskin applying a dark Americana theme (oxblood, aged gold, ink) when Evil Mode is active.
- **UI-03**: Specialized End Screens ranking unconvicted players by their offshore heist tier, or detailing their downfall upon conviction or electoral rejection.

## Tasks
1. **EndScreen Mode Branching (`src/screens/EndScreen.svelte`)**:
   - Detect `session.isEvilMode`:
     - Header: "KLEPTOCRATIC HEIST REVIEW" with term completion / premature termination indicator.
     - Headline Score Card:
       - Displays "Final Offshore Stash" (e.g., `$42.5B Extracted` or `$0.0B Forfeited`).
       - Tagline: "Taxpayer loot successfully laundered into secret foreign numbered accounts" vs "All corrupt proceeds seized by the Department of Justice upon Senate conviction".
       - Breakdowns: Final Heat level, Quarters in power, Senate trial tally (if impeached).
     - Navigation & Tabs:
       - Add a dedicated "Extraction Ledger" / "Corrupt Levers" tab (`data-testid="tab-evil-levers"`):
         - Lists all corrupt levers enacted, siphoned funds per lever, and investigation heat generated.
     - Zone 4 Historian Verdict:
       - Uses `classifyKleptocratVerdict(session)` from Plan 20-01.
       - Renders distinctive Heist Tier badge (`data-testid="kleptocrat-tier-badge"`):
         - `DISGRACED CONVICT`, `OUSTED GRIFTER`, `PETTY GRIFTER`, `GILDED OLIGARCH`, `SOVEREIGN PLUNDERER`, `TRANSNATIONAL KLEPTOCRAT`.
       - Satirical debrief and epilogue (exile estate, diplomatic immunity, prison laundry duty).
2. **Unit Tests (`src/screens/EndScreen.test.ts`)**:
   - Add test cases verifying Evil Mode EndScreen renders kleptocrat tier, offshore stash headline, and corrupt ledger.
