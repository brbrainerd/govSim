## Phase Details

### Phase 25: Dramatic SVG Art Overhaul & Reactive Scenes
- **Goal**: Transform govSim's character and scene illustrations from minimal geometric shapes into expressive, layered Americana editorial-poster art that reacts visibly to economic health, crises, approval, and Evil Mode.
- **Plans**: `25-01-PLAN.md`, `25-02-PLAN.md`
- **Accomplishments**:
  - Upgraded `Character.svelte` with hairstyles, ears, noses, eyebrows, expressive eyes, collars, lapels, ties/pearls, shading, and 5 distinct mood states (`confident`, `stressed`, `scheming`, `panicked`, `triumphant`).
  - Made `OvalOfficeScene.svelte`, `ReporterScene.svelte`, `CourtBench.svelte`, and `FedPortrait.svelte` reactive to live `GameState` (weather/sky, desk bill stacks, crisis phone, Evil Mode shredder and offshore ledger).

### Phase 26: Visual Flair, Micro-Interactions, Satirical Wit & Calm Mode
- **Goal**: Elevate tactile responsiveness and satirical charm with stamp-slam and seat-flip animations, ironic news chyrons and advisor banter, and an accessible Calm Mode toggle.
- **Plans**: `26-01-PLAN.md`, `26-02-PLAN.md`
- **Accomplishments**:
  - Added stamp-slam animations on bill/EO signing and hemicycle seat-flip pulse animations when whipping coalitions.
  - Built `src/content/chyrons.ts` with 40+ state-reactive satirical news chyrons passing the Flesch-Kincaid grade $\le 9.0$ and party-blind gates.
  - Added `"Calm mode"` toggle in `StatusBar.svelte` persisted in `localStorage` and respecting `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Phase 27: Year-1 Progressive Onboarding & Guided Turn Flow
- **Goal**: Implement the progressive Year-1 onboarding journey from `docs/design.md` §7b so new players learn core loops step-by-step without overwhelm, while allowing experienced players to unlock all screens immediately.
- **Plans**: `27-01-PLAN.md`
- **Accomplishments**:
  - Implemented progressive screen unlocking (Turn 1 Oval Office/Budget/Congress, Turn 2 Your Powers, Turn 3+ Voters/Social/Trade/Reforms) with contextual callouts and `NEW` badges.
  - Added `"Unlock all screens"` toggle on `NewGame.svelte` and `Rail.svelte`.

### Phase 28: "Show Your Work" Model Inspector & Citation Audit
- **Goal**: Fulfill the transparency promise of `docs/design.md` §4 with an interactive Model Inspector showing every live equation, coefficient, range, and citation in plain language.
- **Plans**: `28-01-PLAN.md`
- **Accomplishments**:
  - Built `src/content/model-inspector.ts` and `src/ui/ModelInspectorModal.svelte` cataloging 100% of parameters across `COEFF`, `SCORE`, `LIMITS`, and `RULES` plus 6 core macroeconomic equations across 6 domains.
  - Wired entry points in `Economy.svelte` and `AboutModal.svelte`.

### Phase 29: Free AI Director, OpenRouter `:free` Auto-Routing & Living World Variance
- **Goal**: Allow govSim to adapt generatively and non-deterministically to changing circumstances via a Living World variance mode and an opt-in Free AI Director powered by OpenRouter `:free` auto-discovery, OAuth PKCE, browser-local AI, or free-tier BYOK keys.
- **Plans**: `29-01-PLAN.md`, `29-02-PLAN.md`, `29-03-PLAN.md`
- **Accomplishments**:
  - Added Living World mode (`turnEntropy` on stream 19/20) with player/autopilot shock parity and 100% bit-identical replayability.
  - Built `src/app/ai-providers.ts` and `AiSettingsModal.svelte` with OpenRouter `:free` live model discovery, 429 cascade failover, 1-click OAuth PKCE, `window.ai`, Gemini Flash, Groq, and local Ollama support.
  - Built `src/app/ai-director.ts` and `DynamicCrisisModal.svelte` with base64-guarded nonpartisan sanitization and schema-clamped dynamic crisis options.
