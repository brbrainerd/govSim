# Milestone v0.8 Audit: Interactive Myth-Busting, Mirage Levers & Intrinsic Civic Pedagogy

## Executive Summary

Milestone v0.8 integrates civics pedagogy intrinsically into the core gameplay loop across 4 phases (Phases 30–33, 4 plans):
1. Expands `src/content/myths.ts` to 12 spectrum-balanced civics myth cards (`4 left`, `4 right`, `4 both`), strictly nonpartisan and sourced from peer-reviewed institutional literature, each with 2-option prediction challenges, tempting populist mirage copy, and actionable tactical pivot targets.
2. Converts `MythModal.svelte` into an interactive Reality-Check Dossier with optional 1-click bottleneck predictions, tactile `MYTH BUSTED` stamp-slam animations, instant skip/claim paths, and 1-click tactical pivot buttons that stage real constitutional levers and navigate to destination screens.
3. Places tempting populist "Mirage Levers" across 6 policy screens (`Economy`, `Budget`, `Congress`, `Powers`, `Trade`, `Voters`) and adds contextual Chief of Staff macro blame triggers on `OvalOffice.svelte` that surface unexamined myths when inflation, unemployment, or deficits spike.
4. Adds a pure-engine "Debunk vs. Spin" mechanic in `powers.ts` with registered coefficients, approval rallies, trust penalties, Evil Mode heat deltas, and bit-identical preservation of all 8 golden determinism replays, plus a responsive Civic Mastery Dossier on `EndScreen.svelte` with pedagogical archetypes and unlockable badges (`truth_teller`, `demagogue`).

All 4 phases (30–33) have been completed and verified. 10/10 requirements are satisfied with 0 gaps, 0 orphans, and 0 critical debt.

## 3. Cross-Phase Integration & Flows

1. **Mirage Discovery Flow**:
   - Player on `Economy` clicks `⚡ Order 2% Fed Rate Cut` → opens `MythModal` for `interest_rates`.
   - Player tests prediction → reveals institutional reality and Federal Reserve dual mandate.
   - Player clicks tactical pivot button `Adjust Top Bracket Tax` → modal closes, stages `tax_brackets` draft in Session, and navigates to `Budget` screen.
2. **Debunk vs. Spin Flow**:
   - Player clicks `📺 SPIN THIS MYTH (+2% Approval, −Trust)` on `gas_prices`.
   - Staged tray shows `1 staged (TV Spin: Gas Prices)`.
   - Turn advances → engine applies `+2%` approval rally for 1 turn, `-1.5` permanent trust penalty, and `+5` heat in Evil Mode.
   - At term end, `EndScreen.svelte` Civic Mastery Dossier accurately reflects 1 Spun myth, calculates trust drag, and categorizes the player's pedagogical archetype.
