# Plan 16-03: Dedicated Trade & Tariffs Screen UI with Accessible Fallbacks (TRADE-01, TRADE-02, TRADE-03)

## Objective
Build and mount the dedicated `Trade` screen (`src/screens/Trade.svelte`) in the application navigation, wire draft trade actions into `Session`, and provide interactive controls for strategic sector tariffs, bilateral trade agreements, trade balance metrics, and state export exposure tables.

## Scope
1. **Navigation & Icon Registration (`src/design/icons.ts`, `src/app/screens.ts`)**:
   - Add `globe: Globe` icon to `src/design/icons.ts`.
   - Register `{ id: 'trade', label: 'Trade & Tariffs', icon: 'globe', group: 'act', unlockTurn: 2, question: 'How should America trade with the world?' }` in `src/app/screens.ts`.
2. **Session Draft Actions (`src/app/session.svelte.ts`)**:
   - Add `trade: TradeDraft` to session draft.
   - Implement `toggleSectorTariff(id: SectorTariffId)` and `toggleTradeAgreement(id: TradeAgreementId)`.
   - Derive trade balances and retaliatory rates.
3. **Dedicated Trade Screen (`src/screens/Trade.svelte`, `src/screens/Game.svelte`)**:
   - Create `src/screens/Trade.svelte` with:
     - Trade balance metrics strip: Exports, Imports, Net Deficit, Consumer Price Pass-Through, Foreign Retaliatory Rate.
     - Strategic Sector Tariffs section (chips, minerals, cleanEnergy) with Section 301/232 statutory badges and toggles.
     - Bilateral Trade Agreements section (EU, Indo-Pacific, Americas) with clout costs, retaliatory mitigation, and ratify toggles.
     - State Export Exposure visualization and accessible table fallback (`TRADE-03`).
   - Mount `Trade` screen in `src/screens/Game.svelte`.
4. **Unit Testing (`src/screens/Trade.test.ts`)**:
   - Verify screen rendering, interactive toggle triggers, accessible tables, and button affordances.
