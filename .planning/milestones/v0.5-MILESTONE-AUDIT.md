## 3. Cross-Phase Integration & E2E Flows

Integration testing across the 4 phases was validated by the comprehensive Playwright test suite (`tests/evil-mode.spec.ts`, `tests/evil-levers.spec.ts`, `tests/impeachment.spec.ts`, `tests/heist-reskin.spec.ts`), verifying 15 end-to-end integration specs across desktop viewports (`1376x768` and `1280x720`):

1. **Setup & Mode Isolation**:
   - `NewGame.svelte` Evil Mode toggle cleanly initializes `session.isEvilMode` and `EvilState`. Standard game mode displays no evil UI artifacts, preserving pure nonpartisan civics experience.
2. **Theme Switching & Visual Reskin**:
   - Dark Americana theme (`oxblood`, `aged gold`, `ink`) activates via reactive `$effect` applying `data-theme="evil"` on `document.documentElement`.
   - WCAG AA color contrast verified across all buttons (`.stamp`, `.btn`, `.corrupt-levers-btn`) and labels (`.evil-intel-tag`).
3. **Corrupt Siphoning & Watchdog Suppression**:
   - Enacting crony procurement (`crony_contracts`) and land auctions (`auction_public_lands`) correctly routes kickbacks into `offshoreStashBn` while generating heat.
   - Watchdog purges (`fire_inspectors_general`) and law enforcement pressure suppress heat while levying heavy trust penalties.
4. **Institutional Retaliation Flow**:
   - Heat > 60 triggers special counsel investigations and subpoena warnings in `StatusBar.svelte`, siphoning 25 clout/quarter.
   - Heat > 80 triggers `ImpeachmentModal.svelte`, executing pure arithmetic House floor vote (218 majority threshold) and Senate trial vote (67 supermajority threshold).
5. **Endgame Branching**:
   - **Acquitted / Unconvicted**: Reaching end-of-term shows Kleptocratic Heist Review with live stash, Extraction Ledger breakdown, and 6-tier satirical classification (`classifyKleptocratVerdict`).
   - **Convicted Downfall**: Senate conviction triggers immediate 100% asset forfeiture to `$0.0B`, marks term terminated, and presents the `Disgraced Convict` downfall epilogue.
