# Phase 44 Plan 01: Quarterly Mandate & Task Force RNG Draft — Brotato Shop Loop

## Objective
Implement the deterministic *Brotato*-style White House Task Force & Mandate RNG draft engine (`PERK-01`), curated 24-card nonpartisan catalog (`PERK-03`), interactive `PerkDraftModal.svelte` with Clout rerolls, card lock, and skip-for-Clout (`PERK-02`), and `Classic Sim (No Perks)` toggle on `NewGame.svelte`.

## Tasks
1. **Engine Stream, Coefficients, Trace & Catalog (`PERK-01`, `PERK-03`)**:
   - Add `perks: 21` to `STREAM` in `src/engine/rng.ts`.
   - Register perk draft coefficients in `src/engine/coefficients.ts` and constants in `src/engine/units.ts`.
   - Add `'clout:perks'` and `'perk:'` prefix in `src/engine/trace.ts`, `src/engine/modifiers.ts`, and `src/content/text/why.ts`.
   - Create `src/content/perks.ts` with 24 nonpartisan Task Force cards across Tiers I–IV and 6 Policy Doctrine families, all passing Flesch-Kincaid grade ≤ 9.0.
   - Create `src/engine/perks.ts` with pure deterministic `rollPerkSlate`, `perkRerollCost`, and `resolvePerkDecision`, wired into `src/engine/decisions.ts` and `src/engine/game.ts`.
2. **UI Modal, Session & Classic Sim Toggle (`PERK-02`)**:
   - Wire `PerkDecision` and `classicSim?: boolean` into `src/app/session.svelte.ts` and `src/app/save.ts`.
   - Create `src/ui/PerkDraftModal.svelte` with 3-card slate, Tier badges, Doctrine family pills, `TradeoffPill`, Lock toggle, Spend Clout to Reroll, and Skip for +Clout.
   - Add `"Classic Sim (No Perks)"` toggle on `src/screens/NewGame.svelte` and Task Force Draft launcher/status on `src/screens/OvalOffice.svelte` and `src/App.svelte`.
   - Add unit tests in `src/engine/perks.test.ts` and `src/ui/PerkDraftModal.test.ts`.
