# Phase 45 Plan 01: Policy Doctrine Synergy Tags & Chain-Reaction Engine

## Objective
Implement Policy Doctrine Tags across executive orders, budget levers, social policies, trade pacts/tariffs, institutional reforms, and drafted Task Forces (`SYN-01`), the *How Many Dudes?*-inspired `2 / 4 / 6` set thresholds and deterministic chain-reaction triggers in `src/engine/synergies.ts` (`SYN-02`), and the live `SynergyBar.svelte` with cascading combo popups on `OvalOffice.svelte` and `TurnReport.svelte` (`SYN-03`).

## Tasks
1. **Create `src/engine/synergies.ts` (`SYN-01`, `SYN-02`)**:
   - Map Executive Orders (`eo_*`), Trade Agreements (`pact_*`), Sector Tariffs (`tariff_*`), Institutional Reforms (`ref_*`, `amendment_*`), Evil Levers (`evil_*`), Budget Levers, and Social Levers to `DoctrineTag[]` (`getActionDoctrineTags`).
   - Compute active Doctrine counts across `activePerks`, `activeOrders`, `trade.agreements`, `trade.sectorTariffs`, `activeReforms`, `ratifiedAmendments`, active Budget deviations (`inf`, `rd`, `ed`, `mcd`, `mil`, `tar`), and active Social policies.
   - Evaluate `2 / 4 / 6` set thresholds (`SYNERGY_THRESHOLD_1`, `SYNERGY_THRESHOLD_2`, `SYNERGY_THRESHOLD_3`) and event-driven chain-reaction triggers when a turn's enacted bill, signed EO, trade pact, reform, or drafted perk shares a Doctrine family at `>= 2` count.
   - Record all set bonuses and chain triggers into `report.trace` (`synergy:<tag>`, `clout:synergy`) and `report.log` (`kind: 'synergy'`).
2. **Surface Doctrine Tag Badges & `SynergyBar.svelte` (`SYN-01`, `SYN-03`)**:
   - Build `src/ui/SynergyBar.svelte` (`data-testid="synergy-bar"`) showing active Doctrine family counts, `2/4/6` tier badges, and sequential combo popups (`data-testid="synergy-chain-popup"`) respecting `Calm mode`.
   - Mount `SynergyBar.svelte` on `src/screens/OvalOffice.svelte` and `src/ui/TurnReport.svelte` (hidden when `session.isClassicSim`).
   - Surface compact Doctrine tag pills on `src/screens/Powers.svelte` and `src/screens/Trade.svelte`.
3. **Tests & Verification**:
   - Add `src/engine/synergies.test.ts` and `src/ui/SynergyBar.test.ts`.
   - Run `pnpm test && pnpm guard && pnpm check`.
