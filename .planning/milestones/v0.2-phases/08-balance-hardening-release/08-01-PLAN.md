<id>08-01-01</id>
<title>Define 12 Policy Milestone Badges and Evaluation Engine</title>
<read_first>
- src/engine/game.ts
- src/content/text/
</read_first>
<action>
1. Create `src/content/badges.ts`:
   - Define `Badge` interface: `id: string`, `title: string`, `category: 'fiscal' | 'crises' | 'powers' | 'electoral' | 'stewardship'`, `description: string`, `criterion: string`, `icon: string`.
   - Implement the 12 milestone badges:
     1. `balanced_budget`: "Fiscal Discipline" (Brought federal deficit under 1.0% of GDP).
     2. `full_employment`: "Full Employment" (Raised people working ratio above 62.0%).
     3. `inflation_tamed`: "Inflation Tamed" (Inflation rate brought within 2.0% ± 0.5%).
     4. `debt_stabilized`: "Debt Stabilized" (Reduced debt-to-GDP ratio by >= 2.0 percentage points from baseline).
     5. `crisis_pilot`: "Crisis Pilot" (Successfully resolved 3 or more crises without severe approval drop).
     6. `restrained_executive`: "Executive Restraint" (Signed at least 3 executive orders with 0 overturned by the Supreme Court).
     7. `judicial_appointment`: "The Bench" (Appointed a Supreme Court justice confirmed by the Senate).
     8. `bipartisan_builder`: "Coalition Builder" (Passed 3 or more legislative bills through Congress).
     9. `master_orator`: "Bully Pulpit" (Delivered speeches to all 3 audiences: General Public, Congress, and Business).
     10. `electoral_mandate`: "Electoral Mandate" (Won presidential reelection with >= 300 electoral votes).
     11. `social_trust`: "High Trust" (Maintained social trust at or above 25.0%).
     12. `transformational_legacy`: "Transformational Presidency" (Achieved +5.0 or higher Prosperity Index score vs doing nothing).
2. Create `src/engine/badges.ts`:
   - Pure function `evaluateBadges(state: GameState, history: GameState[], options?: { party?: string, speechesGiven?: string[], billsPassedCount?: number, scotusConfirmed?: boolean }): string[]` returning array of unlocked badge IDs.
   - Pure function `checkBadgeUnlock(badgeId: string, state: GameState, history: GameState[], meta: any): boolean`.
3. Create `src/engine/badges.test.ts` and `src/content/badges.test.ts`:
   - Verify every badge has valid copy, non-empty description, reading grade <= 9, and correct predicate logic.
</action>

<action>
1. Update `src/app/session.svelte.ts`:
   - Track `unlockedBadges: string[] = $state([])`.
   - Track `newlyUnlockedBadges: string[] = $state([])` (badges unlocked on the most recent turn).
   - Track auxiliary metadata required for badge evaluation: `speechesGiven: Set<'general' | 'congress' | 'business'>`, `passedBillsCount: number`, `scotusConfirmed: boolean`.
   - In `nextTurn()`:
     - Run `evaluateBadges(next, this.history, meta)`.
     - Calculate diff between old `unlockedBadges` and newly calculated badges.
     - Store newly unlocked badges in `this.newlyUnlockedBadges`.
     - Update `this.unlockedBadges`.
   - In `retryTurn16()` and `reset()`:
     - Re-evaluate badges for the restored snapshot so state remains strictly deterministic.
</action>
