<action>
Create `src/ui/ElectionNightModal.svelte`:
- Accepts props: `outcome: ElectionOutcome`, `autopilotOutcome: ElectionOutcome`, `onclose: () => void`, `onretry: () => void`.
- Animated sequence:
  - Batches safe states immediately (first 1.5s).
  - Calls battleground swing states sequentially with dramatic pacing (up to 4s total).
  - Updates running EV counts for player and opponent in real time.
  - Highlights tipping-point state with gold star indicator.

  - "Skip to Final Result [Space]" button.
  - Automatically detect `window.matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)')`. If matched, immediately skip all intervals/delays and render final tally instantly.
- Milestone announcement:
  - Clear announcement: "PROJECTED WINNER: YOU" or "PROJECTED WINNER: OPPONENT" (or "269–269 TIE: HOUSE TO DECIDE").
  - Side-by-side comparison pill with autopilot outcome.
  - Action button: "Continue to Second Term" or "View Legacy Record".
  - "Retry Turn 16" action button.
</action>
