## 3. Cross-Phase Integration & User Flows

1. **New Game Scenario Selection → Inauguration Briefing**: Choosing a scenario (*Standard Baseline*, *70s Stagflation Shock*, *Debt Ceiling Brinkmanship*, *Global Trade War*) overrides initial macro and fiscal conditions deterministically, passes through `applyScenarioStart`, displays the strategic context card on the Oval Office, and preserves the badge on the EndScreen and deflate-raw `#run=` hash.
2. **Oval Office Chief of Staff → Advisor Plays**: Chief of Staff recommendations surface `Play: [Action]` pills that navigate to target screens (Budget, Congress, Powers, Crisis, Myths), pre-fill recommended budget packages, and auto-whip coalitions with one click.
3. **Congress Auto-Whip Helper**: `findCheapestCoalition` dynamically computes the cheapest deal subset needed to reach 218 House votes, respecting active social drafts and heat friction penalties without violating engine purity.
4. **Budget Household Persona & In-Force Baseline**: Kitchen-table budget impact reflects `session.selectedHouseholdPersona`, switching personas updates monthly dollar deltas immediately, and sliders visually distinguish in-force enacted policy from the 2026 baseline.
5. **Turn Pacing & Fast-Forward**: Top-bar Staged Decisions tray reflects queued actions with jump buttons; fast-forward skips quiet quarters and interrupts reliably on crises, annual deadlines, midterms, or reelection night.
6. **Save/Share Trust Boundary**: Schema validation round-trips all decisions (including `geopoliticalCrisis`, `foreignPolicy`, `trade`, `evil`, and explainer myth views) with bit-identical hash verification.
