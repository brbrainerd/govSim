# Requirements: govSim (Milestone v0.5)

**Defined:** 2026-10-12
**Core Value:** A player can make real presidential tradeoffs (or play the satirical "Evil Mode" kleptocracy) against an honest, explainable model of the current US, and always understand *why* the country got better or worse.

## v0.5 Requirements

### Evil Mode Game Engine & Heat Mechanics (EVIL)

- [x] **EVIL-01**: Player can toggle "Evil Mode" at New Game, setting the primary objective to maximizing an offshore stash ($B) while evading conviction.
- [x] **EVIL-02**: Pure engine computes a dynamic 0–100 Heat meter driven by corrupt actions, leaking evidence, and media scrutiny, with slow natural decay.
- [x] **EVIL-03**: Heat level applies escalating penalties to public trust, voter approval, and congressional legislative friction.

### Corrupt Executive Levers & Cronyism (LEVER)

- [x] **LEVER-01**: Player can award no-bid crony procurement contracts and auction federal land/assets to siphon kickbacks directly into the offshore stash.
- [x] **LEVER-02**: Player can sell targeted tariff exemptions and solicit presidential "library foundation" donations at the cost of heat generation.
- [x] **LEVER-03**: Player can fire inspectors general and pressure law enforcement to suppress active heat generation at high clout and trust costs.

### Institutional Backlash & Impeachment (IMPEACH)

- [x] **IMPEACH-01**: High heat levels (>60) trigger special counsel investigations and congressional subpoenas that drain presidential clout each quarter.
- [x] **IMPEACH-02**: Critical heat levels (>80) trigger House impeachment votes using the 435-seat congressional bloc voting model.
- [x] **IMPEACH-03**: Impeachment leads to a Senate conviction trial requiring a 2/3 supermajority; conviction instantly terminates the term and forfeits the entire offshore stash.

### Satirical Visual Reskin & Heist End Screens (UI)

- [x] **UI-01**: Satirical visual reskin applying a dark Americana theme (oxblood, aged gold, ink) when Evil Mode is active.
- [x] **UI-02**: Live Offshore Stash counter and Heat Gauge integrated into the status bar and Oval Office / Situation Room.
- [x] **UI-03**: Specialized End Screens ranking unconvicted players by their offshore heist tier, or detailing their downfall upon conviction or electoral rejection.

## Out of Scope

| Feature | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| Real foreign heads of state | Violates civics nonpartisanship and fictional person policy (Q6) |
| Direct tactical military combat sim | govSim is a presidential civilian strategy and macro civics game, not an RTS or wargame |
| Nuclear war annihilation sandbox | Terminal instant-lose state without pedagogical civics value |
| Live geopolitical news feeds | Requires external server, breaches zero-egress CSP and creates partisan risk (E5) |
