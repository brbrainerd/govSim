# Milestone v0.7 Audit: Dramatic Visuals, Satirical Wit, Progressive Onboarding, Model Inspector & Free AI Director

## Executive Summary

Milestone v0.7 ("Dramatic Visuals, Satirical Wit, Progressive Onboarding, Model Inspector & Free AI Director") elevates govSim's artistic expression, satirical charm, pedagogical transparency, and generative adaptability across 5 phases (Phases 25–29, 9 plans). It upgrades all character and scene illustrations into layered, state-reactive Americana editorial-poster SVG art, adds stamp-slam and hemicycle seat-flip micro-interactions alongside a Calm Mode toggle and satirical news-ticker chyrons, guides new players progressively through Year 1 with an Unlock All toggle, exposes all 138 model parameters and equations in an interactive "Show Your Work" Model Inspector, and introduces an optional non-deterministic Living World variance mode plus a Free AI Game Director (`openrouter/free` + `:free` auto-discovery, 1-click OAuth PKCE, Chrome `window.ai`, Gemini/Groq/Ollama BYOK) generating party-blind briefings and schema-clamped dynamic crisis dilemmas.

All 5 phases (25–29) comprising 9 plans have been executed and verified. The 3-source cross-reference across `REQUIREMENTS.md`, phase `*-VERIFICATION.md` reports, and `*-SUMMARY.md` artifacts confirms 100% coverage (14/14 requirements satisfied) with zero gaps, zero orphans, and zero critical technical debt. Furthermore, the cross-phase code review (`29-REVIEW.md`) evaluated all 48 modified files across 5 dimensions and resolved all identified findings (including AI dynamic crisis clout deduction, Living World autopilot entropy parity, token compliance, and share URL option round-tripping).
