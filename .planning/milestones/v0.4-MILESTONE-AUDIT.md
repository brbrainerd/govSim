## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone v0.4 ("Foreign Policy & International Crises") equips the player with full presidential international authorities:
1. **Geopolitical Crises Deck & Escalation (Phase 13)**: Paced international events (oil embargo, shipping strait blockade, foreign debt contagion, cyber infrastructure attack) with multi-turn countdowns, cited diplomatic/economic/military triads, persistent Oval Office review alerts, and identical autopilot streaming.
2. **Foreign Policy & Sanctions Levers (Phase 14)**: Defense readiness posture slider, foreign assistance adjustments, targeted economic sanctions with domestic inflation pass-through, and the "Cut Foreign Aid to Fix Deficit" civics myth card.
3. **Security Index & Macro Feedback Engine (Phase 15)**: Pure deterministic 0–100 Security Index based on defense readiness, alliance strength, and crisis containment, driving the 10% Security weight in the composite Prosperity Index with causal trace explanations.
4. **International Trade & Tariffs Screen (Phase 16)**: Dedicated Trade & Tariffs screen with strategic sector tariffs (chips, minerals, clean energy) citing Section 301 and Section 232, bilateral trade accords with reciprocal mitigation, and an accessible 10-state export vulnerability matrix.
