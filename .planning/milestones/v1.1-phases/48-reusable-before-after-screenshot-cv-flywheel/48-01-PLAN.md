# Plan 48-01: Reusable Before/After Screenshot Computer-Vision Flywheel & Baseline Capture

## Objective
Create `scripts/cv-flywheel.ts`, a reusable TypeScript computer-vision screenshot & telemetry flywheel CLI that:
1. Launches/connects to the Vite dev server and captures headless Playwright screenshots across `1376×768` and `1280×720` for key screens (`newgame`, `oval-day`, `oval-evil`, `powers`, `congress`, `budget`, `economy`, `voters`).
2. Computes pixel-level regional luminance/palette metrics (via Chromium `<canvas>` `getImageData`) and DOM/SVG computer-vision telemetry (card vs. paper luminance contrast, character outer-stroke counts, `.chyron-ticker` SVG overlap, above-the-fold NPC visibility on `Powers`, White House hero banner dimensions on `NewGame`, and raw Unicode emoji counts).
3. Generates side-by-side **BEFORE | AFTER | DESIGN TARGET** composite PNG comparison sheets and a structured Markdown + JSON report (`test-results/cv-flywheel/cv-report.md` and `cv-report.json`).

## Acceptance Criteria
- `node scripts/cv-flywheel.ts --stage before` captures all baseline screenshots and `before/metrics.json`.
- `node scripts/cv-flywheel.ts --stage after` captures post-change screenshots and `after/metrics.json`.
- `node scripts/cv-flywheel.ts --compare` produces composite **Before | After | Target** PNGs and `cv-report.md`.
